# GIS for Renewable Energy Planning

Three spatial analyses for renewable energy projects in ArcGIS Pro (Spatial Analyst): connecting a wind farm to the grid, comparing the visual impact of two wind farm sites, and estimating solar irradiation on roof surfaces. All three study areas lie in North Rhine-Westphalia.

Each analysis follows the same structure: question, data and source, method, result, analytical limits. Each folder contains the description and the map as PDF.

**[Full portfolio (PDF, 10 pages)](piccillo_gis_portfolio_renewables_2026.pdf)**

## Analyses

| # | Analysis | Question | Tools |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | [Cable route](01-cable-route/) | Least-cost grid connection for a wind farm, with three connection points compared | ArcGIS Pro, Spatial Analyst, arcpy |
| 02 | [Wind farm visibility](02-wind-farm-visibility/) | Which of two wind farm sites has less visual impact on recreation areas? | ArcGIS Pro, Spatial Analyst |
| 03 | [Rooftop solar irradiation](03-rooftop-solar/) | How much winter sun reaches each roof surface? | ArcGIS Pro, Spatial Analyst |

## Background and attribution

The analyses are based on exercises from the "GIS and Geodata Expert" programme at GIS-Akademie Berlin (2026), which set the tasks, parameters and scenario data. I carried out the cable route and wind farm analyses independently; the solar analysis was demonstrated in a guided session and repeated on my own. The course scripts and exercise datasets belong to GIS-Akademie GmbH and are **not** included here. Open data used in the exercises are cited in each folder with their licence. Data checks, maps, scripts and assessments are my own work.
